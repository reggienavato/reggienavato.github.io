IGNITE: Automation Anywhere Partner PowerUp Bot Camp

September 11, 2026, Friday | Automation Anywhere Automation (A360)

A partner enablement program focused on hands-on Automation Anywhere Automation 360 training, including bot building, business rules application, and technical enablement.

Key Learning Areas

  • Automation 360 bot development and workflow design
  • Business rules and process automation
  • Applying automation to practical business scenarios
  • Automation architecture and implementation considerations
  • Technical enablement for partner-led solution delivery

Learning Takeaway

The hands-on sessions provided a valuable opportunity to gain practical experience and a better understanding of how automation can be applied to actual business processes and day-to-day operational requirements. Beyond the technical aspects, the sessions helped me appreciate how the right automation approach can simplify tasks, improve efficiency, and support better business outcomes. One of the key takeaways from the experience is the importance of understanding the business process first how the work is currently being done, where the challenges are, and which areas can benefit from automation. From there, the appropriate technology and solution can be considered based on the actual business need, rather than simply applying technology for the sake of automation.

I am grateful to VSTECS for providing this learning opportunity and for making the sessions practical, engaging, and relevant to real-world business scenarios. The knowledge and experience gained from the hands-on activities will be valuable in my work, particularly in understanding how technology can be aligned more effectively with business requirements and operational goals. Many Thanks to MDTI for this opportunity to learn and to VSTECS, for sharing your expertise and for creating an environment where participants can learn not only about the technology, futhermore, on how to apply this technology into a meaningful and practical way.
